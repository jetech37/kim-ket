A little about me

Hi, I'm Kim Ket

I was born and raised in Cambodia and moved to the United States in my late teens, carrying with me a deep love for the practices that have shaped my life. For more than ten years I have taught yoga and meditation, shared Reiki, and read tarot for people moving through change.

My path led me to Rishikesh, India, where I completed 500 hours of training in Hatha and Ashtanga-Vinyasa yoga at Rishikesh Yogpeeth, studying close to its roots and learning to truly listen, to the breath, to the body, to the quiet wisdom underneath. As a certified trauma-informed yoga teacher, I make every session gentle: we move at your pace, with your comfort and consent held at the center.

I am also a medical assistant by profession and an Integrative Ayurvedic Technician, so I hold both worlds with care: the practical and the intuitive, the clinical and the soulful. I believe healing is not about fixing what is broken. It is about remembering what is already steady underneath the noise.

A world traveler, tree hugger and entrepreneur, I love bringing people together as a community: creating safe, healing spaces where we can connect, restore and grow.
